What happens at a night

01

Open trading

Bring your binder, find your missing cards, make fair trades face to face.

02

Live pack openings

We rip packs on the mic. Every big pull gets a cheer.

03

Best Pull contest

Show your best card of the month. The room votes, the winner will "RIP" a donated pack.

04

Vendor tables

Local sellers with singles, slabs and sealed product.

House rules

  • Trades are between members. Check your cards before you shake on it.
  • No fakes. Knowingly trading one gets you banned.
  • It's all ages. Keep it friendly for the kids at the table.
  • Alcohol is 21+ with ID, per The Canopy Club's rules.
